### What was decided by us vs generated by AI
- **Our decisions:** choice of the two strategies, the crowded-carry hypothesis, downloading FRED data by hand, the reduced crypto universe and USDC check, and what to present.
- **AI-generated:** the code in `code/`, the summary tables and text drafts, the page and slide drafts. All numbers come from running the scripts; none were typed in by hand.
- **Checks we did on AI output:** the matched CFTC contract list, the USDC sanity check (price 0.985-1.002, funding ~0%), FTT/LUNA delisting dates, and the 0.82 correlation of Strategy A with the course's carry factor.
